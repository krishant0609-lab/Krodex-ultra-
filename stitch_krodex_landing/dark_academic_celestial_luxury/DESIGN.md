## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes dark academic gravitas with celestial luxury and sharp modern engineering. Designed for high-stakes intelligence, wealth architecture, and research platforms, it projects timeless authority, quiet affluence, and intellectual rigor.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Classicism** (scholarly, high-contrast serif typography and generous, deliberate hierarchy) with **Modern Tonal Glassmorphism** (semi-translucent surfaces, sapphire-tinted obsidian depths, tactile hairline borders, and subtle cosmic illumination). 

The emotional response should feel like entering an exclusive nocturnal observatory or a subterranean private archive: silent, focused, exquisitely tailored, and profoundly discerning. Interactions reject neon flashiness in favor of measured gold foil glints, deep sapphire ambient blooms, and soft tactile transitions.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in deep nocturnal space, warm precious metals, and esoteric celestial accents.

### Core Canvas & Surfaces
- **Obsidian Base (Canvas):** `#090D16` transitioning to `#0D1322` for base viewport backgrounds.
- **Surface Level 1 (Panels / Base Containers):** `#111827` (translucent variant: `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.75)`).
- **Surface Level 2 (Elevated Cards / Flyouts):** `#161F33` (translucent variant: `rgba(22, 31, 51, 0.8)`).
- **Surface Level 3 (Active States / Interactive Cells):** `#1E293B`.
- **Structural Borders & Dividers:** Delicate hairline stroke `#1F2A44` (or `rgba(31, 42, 68, 0.7)`). High-emphasis border: `rgba(229, 201, 146, 0.35)`.

### Accent Spectrum
- **Champagne Gold (Primary Accent):** Primary `#E5C992`, Burnished/Metallic `#D4AF37`, Muted Gilded `#B3975A`. Reserved for primary CTAs, active indicators, fine keylines, and pinnacle editorial moments.
- **Celestial Sapphire (Secondary Accent):** Vibrant `#3B82F6`, Deep Cosmic `#2563EB`, Abyssal `#1D4ED8`. Used for system telemetries, links, secondary action nodes, and radial backdrop lighting.
- **Astral Amethyst (Tertiary Accent):** Soft Lavender `#9D8EC2`, Deep Violet `#6B5B95`. Used for esoteric metadata, scholarly tags, AI/algorithmic cues, and tertiary states.

### Typography & Content
- **Primary Text (Warm Ivory):** `#F9F8F6` — delivers an organic parchment-lit warmth against dark slate.
- **Secondary Text (Parchment Muted):** `#C4CDD5` — high legibility for analytical copy and descriptions.
- **Tertiary / Muted (Starlight Dust):** `#717C96` — fine labels, timestamps, structural hints.

### Functional Semantics
- **Success / Yield:** Muted Sage `#34D399` with glow `rgba(52, 211, 153, 0.15)`.
- **Warning / Scarcity:** Amber `#F59E0B` with glow `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`.
- **Destructive / Alert:** Crimson `#F43F5E` with glow `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.15)`.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional dialogue between the classical humanist gravitas of **Newsreader** and the modernist, hyper-legible precision of **Manrope**.

- **Editorial Serifs (Newsreader):** Applied to top-level section headings, landing moments, thematic quotes, and prominent numerical milestones. When set in italic weights, it imbues the experience with archival depth and academic prestige.
- **Geometric Sans (Manrope):** Engineered for all functional UI components, data visualization callouts, dense tabular structures, navigational bars, inputs, and functional copy.
- **Micro-Labels & Classifiers:** Set in `label-caps` (all-caps Manrope with `0.12em` tracking) to mimic the catalog numbering and folio headers of rare manuscripts and luxury watch faces.
- **Number Treatment:** Numerical figures within financial or analytical modules must use tabular alignment (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) with Manrope semi-bold weights for razor-sharp precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an **8-point base grid** governed by architectural symmetry and generous negative space, allowing high-density analytical widgets to breathe naturally alongside editorial introductions.

### Grid Configuration
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column grid, max content width `1280px` (or `1440px` for dense operational dashboards), 24px (`space-lg`) gutters, 64px (`margin-desktop`) outer margin.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid, 20px gutters, 40px (`margin-tablet`) outer margin.
- **Mobile (<= 767px):** 4-column grid, 16px (`gutter-mobile`) gutters, 20px (`margin-mobile`) outer margin.

### Hierarchy & Content Flow
Modules stack logically with vertical separations scaled from `space-xl` (internal container padding) to `space-3xl` or `space-4xl` between major conceptual sections. Asymmetry is permitted exclusively when highlighting luxury hero units (e.g., an 8-column editorial summary paired with a 4-column floating telemetry panel).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this design system is achieved through **luminous cosmic stratification** rather than heavy, muddy drop shadows. Surfaces feel like polished dark glass resting atop quiet abyssal depths, bathed in ethereal light sources.

### Elevation Levels

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#090D16` to `#0D1322` radiant mesh. May contain low-opacity ambient radial gradients (e.g., `radial-gradient(circle at 50% 0%, rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.08) 0%, transparent 70%)`).
- **Level 1 (Structural Sections & Inset Panels):** `#111827` with a delicate outer border `1px solid #1F2A44`. Zero hard shadow; relies strictly on subtle surface contrast.
- **Level 2 (Cards & Floating Modules):** 
  - Background: `rgba(22, 31, 51, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`.
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(31, 42, 68, 0.85)`.
  - Shadow: Dual subtle tier — `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(9, 13, 22, 0.6), 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03) inset`.
- **Level 3 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Focus):**
  - Border: Shifts to `1px solid rgba(229, 201, 146, 0.4)`.
  - Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 24px -2px rgba(229, 201, 146, 0.12)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals, Overlays & Popovers):**
  - Background: `#161F33` (or `rgba(22, 31, 51, 0.95)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`).
  - Border: `1px solid #1F2A44`.
  - Shadow: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85), 0 0 40px -4px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.15)`.

### Tactile Edge Lighting
Cards and panels use a hairline top specular highlight: `linear-gradient(90deg, transparent 0%, rgba(229, 201, 146, 0.25) 50%, transparent 100%)` along the top border to simulate physical edge illumination.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft Architectural (`1`)** shape language. Sharp corners feel overly utilitarian, whereas excessively rounded pill shapes dilute the intellectual, high-craft dark academic aura.

- **Base Radius (4px / `0.25rem`):** Badges, inline tags, small buttons, and checkbox indicators.
- **Medium Radius (8px / `0.5rem`):** Standard interactive buttons, dropdowns, input controls, and contextual menus.
- **Large Radius (12px / `0.75rem`):** Analytical cards, content containers, chart frames, and modular panels.
- **Extra Large Radius (16px / `1.0rem`):** Dialog overlays, hero spotlight banners, and full-screen modals.
- **Pill Exception:** Pure circular avatars and floating micro-status indicator pips (`rounded-full`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Champagne Gold Fill):** 
  - Background: Gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #E5C992 0%, #D4AF37 100%)`.
  - Text: `#090D16`, Manrope bold 13px, tracked `0.02em`.
  - Shadow: `0 2px 10px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`.
  - Hover: Subtle brightness scale `1.05`, glow expands to `0 4px 20px rgba(229, 201, 146, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary (Obsidian Glass Outline):**
  - Background: `rgba(22, 31, 51, 0.6)`.
  - Border: `1px solid #1F2A44`.
  - Text: `#F9F8F6`, Manrope medium 14px.
  - Hover: Border shifts to `rgba(229, 201, 146, 0.5)`, text to `#E5C992`, subtle celestial sapphire tint on background.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, text `#C4CDD5`, hover text `#F9F8F6` with subtle underline or soft ivory highlight.

### Cards & Modular Panels
- Standard cards feature background `#161F33` (semi-translucent with backdrop blur), framed in hairline `#1F2A44`.
- Padding defaults to `space-xl` (24px) for dashboard cards and `space-2xl` (32px) for editorial showcases.
- Top-level cards feature the gold hairline specular rule at the upper edge.

### Chips & Badges
- **Academic Tag:** Pill or soft-radius shape (`4px`). Background `rgba(157, 142, 194, 0.1)`, border `1px solid rgba(157, 142, 194, 0.25)`, text `#9D8EC2`, 11px uppercase tracked.
- **Status Indicator Badges:** Background `rgba(color, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(color, 0.3)`. Contains a 6px glowing dot for live/operational metrics (Sage for nominal, Amber for caution, Crimson for alert).

### Form Inputs & Selects
- Base container: Background `#0D1322`, border `1px solid #1F2A44`, height 44px, text `#F9F8F6`, placeholder `#717C96`.
- Focus state: Border transforms to `#E5C992`, box-shadow `0 0 0 1px #E5C992, 0 0 12px rgba(229, 201, 146, 0.15)`.
- Field labels: Manrope 12px uppercase tracked `#C4CDD5`, positioned 6px above input.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: 18px by 18px.
- Unchecked: Background `#111827`, border `1px solid #1F2A44`, border-radius 4px (checkbox) or circular (radio).
- Checked: Background `#E5C992`, border `#D4AF37`, icon/dot `#090D16`.

### Tabular Lists & Data Rows
- Row dividers: Hairline stroke `1px solid rgba(31, 42, 68, 0.6)`.
- Row hover: Smooth transition to `rgba(22, 31, 51, 0.5)`.
- Header row: Typography `label-caps` in `#717C96`, padding-bottom `space-sm`.

### Custom Specialty Elements
- **Scholarly Pull Quotes:** Newsreader Italic (`headline-md`) in `#F9F8F6`, flanked by an ivory/gold vertical keyline (`2px` solid `#D4AF37`) along the left edge with 20px indent.
- **Celestial Keyline Dividers:** Hairline rule displaying a center gradient fade: `linear-gradient(90deg, transparent 0%, #1F2A44 20%, #E5C992 50%, #1F2A44 80%, transparent 100%)`.
